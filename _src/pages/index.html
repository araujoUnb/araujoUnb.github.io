About me

Adjunct Professor · Faculty of Science and Technology in Engineering (FCTE/FGA) · University of Brasília (UnB)

I am an Adjunct Professor at the University of Brasília (UnB), Faculdade do Gama, where I have been since December 2019. I coordinated the B.Sc. in Electronic Engineering from 2023 to 2025 and I am vice‑coordinator of LabTelecom, the Shared Teaching and Research Laboratory in Telecommunications (LCEPT). I also serve as Associate Editor of IEEE Signal Processing Letters and of Signal Processing Open (Elsevier).

I hold a Ph.D. (2016) and an M.Sc. (2012) in Teleinformatics Engineering from the Federal University of Ceará (UFC) and a B.Sc. in Telecommunications Engineering from the University of Fortaleza (UNIFOR, 2010). During my Ph.D. I spent one year as a visiting researcher at Ericsson Research (Kista, Sweden) in the Radio Access Technology department, and from 2012 to 2019 I was a research engineer in the GTEL / Ericsson Research cooperation program at UFC. I was a CNPq postdoctoral fellow at UFC in 2017–2018.

My work sits at the intersection of statistical and array signal processing and wireless communications: parameter estimation in propagation channels, massive MIMO and MIMO‑OFDM channel estimation, hybrid beamforming, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, localization and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) for 5G/6G, GNSS receivers, LEO satellite links, and software‑defined radio.

Research interests

Array & statistical signal processing

Direction‑of‑arrival and delay estimation, subspace methods, adaptive beamforming, Bayesian estimation, tensor‑based (PARAFAC) modeling and compressive sensing.

Channel estimation for large arrays and RIS

Massive MIMO and MIMO‑OFDM channel estimation, hybrid analog/digital beamforming, beyond‑diagonal reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (BD‑RIS), near‑field propagation.

Localization, sensing and ISAC for 5G/6G

Near‑field user localization, indoor positioning, integrated sensing and communication, automotive FMCW radar signal processing.

GNSS and satellite communications

GNSS time‑delay estimation with antenna arrays, multipath mitigation, adaptive arrays for LEO satellite downlink, low‑cost ground stations.

Machine learning for the physical layer

Reinforcement learning for power control, link adaptation and beam management; unsupervised learning for user grouping.

Software‑defined radio and IoT

SDR implementations of LoRa/Sigfox, chirp spread spectrum, cross‑protocol repeaters, microwave and RF systems.

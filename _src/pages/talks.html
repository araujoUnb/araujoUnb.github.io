Invited talks & seminars

  1. Nov 2026 “BD‑RIS for 6G: Tensor Modeling and Channel Estimation Exploiting Core Sparsity,” LASP Scientific Seminar Series “Intelligent and Reconfigurable Wireless Systems for 6G”, Laboratory of Applied Signal Processing (LASP), Federal University of Ceará, Fortaleza, Brazil. slides flyer
    Abstract

    Beyond-diagonal reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (BD-RIS) interconnect their elements through reconfigurable impedance networks, which increases wave-manipulation flexibility and received power over conventional RIS and makes them attractive for millimeter-wave and sub-terahertz 6G deployments. This flexibility, however, multiplies the number of cascaded channel coefficients, and least-squares estimators require a number of training frames that grows with the group size. Motivated by the limited scattering of high-frequency channels, we build the signal model of a group-connected BD-RIS and show that the training signal admits a Tucker-3 decomposition whose core tensor has only P² nonzero entries, one per path pair. We then exploit this core sparsity in two two-stage estimators: STORM, which recovers the support greedily through orthogonal matching pursuit, and STAR, which recovers it through a MUSIC-like subspace search, both followed by a Kronecker rank-one factorization that returns the angles and gains of each path pair. Both methods are compared through simulations with TRICE adapted to BD-RIS and with an oracle least-squares estimator, in terms of NMSE versus SNR, training overhead, and number of paths, and of processing time versus group size. Finally, we outline extensions to wideband scenarios and hybrid topologies.

    Related paper: Channel Estimation for Beyond Diagonal RIS Exploiting Core Tensor Sparsity, IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, 2026.

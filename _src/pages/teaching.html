Graduate courses

PPGEE0414Adaptive Array Signal Processing
Sensor arrays and spatial filters; space‑time / space‑frequency representations; adaptive beamformers (Delay‑and‑Sum, MVDR/Capon, LMS/NLMS, GSC) and DOA estimation. Python‑based simulations and a final project with oral presentation.		Graduate Program in Electrical Engineering (PPGEE)
PPGINT2019Fundamentals of Digital Signal Acquisition and Processing
Discrete‑time signals and systems; spectral analysis and digital filtering; multirate processing, filter banks and wavelets, sparsity and compressive sensing.		Graduate Program in Systems Integrity (PPGINT)

FGA0119Electromagnetic Theory
Classical electromagnetic theory for engineers: electrostatics (Coulomb and Gauss laws, potential, capacitance, dielectrics), magnetostatics (Biot–Savart and Ampère laws, magnetic forces) and time‑varying fields — Faraday’s law, Maxwell’s equations, and plane‑wave propagation, power, polarization and reflection in material media (60 h). Course page and lecture slides by topic (slides in Portuguese).		current
FGA0092Communication Principles for Engineering
Spectral analysis of signals (Fourier series and transform, spectral densities), analog modulation (AM, FM, PM, PLL) and its performance in noise, analog‑to‑digital conversion (sampling, quantization, PCM, source coding) and baseband digital transmission, with eight laboratory sessions.
